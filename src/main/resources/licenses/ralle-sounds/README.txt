RALLE processed-xylophone UI sounds

These sounds are original procedural synthesis created for RALLE. They do not
contain samples or assets from Minecraft, Wynncraft, Hychat, or another game.
The generated OGG files are distributed under RALLE's GNU GPL v3.0 license.

The reproducible source is tools/generate_xylophone_sounds.py. It models a
soft-mallet bar with rapidly decaying inharmonic modes, attenuated upper
frequencies, no room reverb, and a short UI-oriented tail.
